# Frontier rules: implementation contract

Approved October 24 after the 120-Moon playtest. Applies only to new strategy-version-two saves; missing markers and explicit version one preserve all historical behavior. Economy, prices, raid respite and N/N+1/N+2 travel remain unchanged.

## Variations specified before implementation

- Configuration: new economy-four games select strategy two; explicit strategy one and earlier economies retain existing behavior. Unsupported strategy markers reject creation.
- Integration: capture in N restricts inherited local Faithful through N+3; first unrestricted departure N+4. They defend locally, but cannot depart on offensives or relief. Returning friendly contingents and newly recruited Faithful are not inherited. Local casualties remove restricted/free Faithful proportionally; absent losses do not reduce the restricted local pool. Reacquisition starts a new restriction on the then-local inherited contingent.
- Routing: choose among friendly immediate road neighbors connected by friendly paths to every contributor. Prefer the neighbor contributing the most troops, then stable city ID. Confirmation and departure validate own routes; freeze the chosen road at departure. Disconnected contributions reject atomically. Never inspect enemy commitments for route selection.
- Secrecy: sealed enemy commitments and enemy raid-respite dates are absent from public projection. Neither admission nor departure rejects for hidden target engagement or respite. Actual arrival retains the existing no-refund invalid-objective/respite handling. Own commitments remain in the ledger. Duplicate target engagements are allowed only in strategy two.
- Detection: after departures and work completion, effective watch I detects outbound threats with target and arrival Moon; II also records mission kind and troop band (<50, 50–99, 100–199, 200+). Store dated immutable warning letters and recipient-scoped detection records. Damage cannot erase learned information; new owners receive no old-owner intelligence. No warning for confirmed orders, arrived/returning missions or a missing watch. No hidden combativity or exact opposing count is exposed.
- Encounters: group due outbound missions by departure Moon and undirected frozen road; combine same-side missions once. Only opposite hostile directions meet. Different roads, different departures, one-sided roads and same-house forces do not meet. No city walls, relief, siege or crop protection enters the field comparison.
- Field arithmetic: ratio below 1.25 is inconclusive, both sides lose 6% of the smaller engaged count and return. Ratio at least 1.25 but below 2 is decisive: stronger loses 2%, weaker 12% of the smaller engaged count. Ratio at least 2 is overwhelming: stronger loses none, weaker 4% of the smaller count. Half-up rounding; no negative survivors. The stronger continues its existing mission on the same combat Moon, even if casualties leave it below the launch minimum. A loser returns on its original schedule, without refund or instant reinforcement. Equal strength has no seal-order advantage.
- Reports/audits: exact own casualties and survivors, qualitative enemy casualties only; no public numerical strength. Store separate private field audit records. City combat uses field survivors and does not automatically surrender a destination.
- UI: own accounts expose restriction dates/counts; paid warning details are available in the alert centre; enemy engagement never disables preparation in strategy two. Surveillance descriptions explain the extra detection benefit only for the new strategy. Both languages remain supported.

The independent tests in `tests/test_frontier_rules.py` specify arithmetic boundaries, transition/ownership behavior, routing, privacy, all mission combinations, aggregation, continuation, warnings and compatibility. Additional HTTP tests cover the authorized presentation. Existing fixtures explicitly select the historical strategy when protecting their earlier contract.
