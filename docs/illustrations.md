# Illustration direction

The owner's October 30 drawings define the decorative folio artwork: naive,
frontal scenes, small simplified figures, irregular brown ink contours and a
limited palette. Keep the neutral pale off-white sky, dusty pink ground, muted
blue, sage green, white cloth and brown skin. Do not warm the images toward yellow
or introduce realistic anatomy, lighting, perspective or uniform digital strokes.
Prefer camels when adding animals. Soldier equipment follows the owner's pencil
sketch, with varied spears, swords, tunics, veils and simple knobbed helmets.

Direct, unaltered owner drawings are `agriculture.jpg`, `granary.jpg`,
`fortifications.jpg` and `siege.jpg`. Their native composition is 3:1; detail
frames preserve it, while compact work-list thumbnails remain square excerpts.
The forge stays unchanged at the owner's explicit request.

Three reference-guided generation sheets supply the other folio scenes: the
nine military scenes, nine supporting scenes, and nine city identities. Each
sheet was mechanically extracted into cells before use. `yaara-atlas.png` and
`cityscapes.png` use equal 512×342 cells in a 3×3 grid; standalone military JPEGs
use the same cell size, except army preparation, edited again as a complete 3:1
panorama at the owner's request. Council reuses the meeting-porch city scene. City identity
artwork does not assert installed upgrades. The separate map background and
fortification-marker atlas retain their existing public-information mapping.

Every construction-start news class reuses the generic construction scene in
the atlas's first cell; army departure reuses army preparation. Retain the news
and all saved recipient/outcome mappings. The eight battle images distinguish
the viewer's blue-clad force from terracotta opponents, including the defender's
perspective. Existing forge art, saved game keys and archived report content are
unchanged. The agriculture family is labelled “Développer l’agriculture” in French
and “Develop agriculture” in English; physical building levels retain their names.
